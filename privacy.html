Privacy Policy

App: Light Up My Heart Manager · Last updated: October 11, 2026

What this app does

Light Up My Heart Manager is a personal tool used by its owner, Light, to manage her own Pinterest business account (@lightupmyheart_official). It reads her boards and pins, publishes her own original lifestyle, slow living, travel, and wellness pins on her behalf, and adds descriptive alt text to images and videos for accessibility. The app only takes action with her explicit authorization.

What data is collected

The app accesses only the following, and only within Light's own Pinterest account:

  • Board and pin information needed to manage her pins (titles, descriptions, images/videos, alt text).
  • Basic account profile information returned by the Pinterest API during authorized sessions.

The app does not collect data from any other person or account.

How data is used and shared

  • Data is used solely to manage Light's own Pinterest content as described above.
  • Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs are used.

Data retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed to perform authorized actions. Light may revoke the app's access at any time from her Pinterest settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Light through any of the platforms listed on the Light Up My Heart home page.
