About

Welcome to Light Up My Heart (點亮我心) — a personal sanctuary dedicated to slow living, everyday warmth, and the art of self-encouragement.

Life takes us through many seasons: quiet home-cooked meals, travel adventures, busy moving days, or simply grabbing a comforting takeout when time is short. Through it all, this space is a reminder that life doesn't have to be perfect; unwinding is a way of loving life.

Here, I share bilingual visual notes, lifestyle moments, and daily quotes to inspire you to find the little things that cheer you up on an ordinary day — because even on the toughest days, a spark of warmth can change everything.
